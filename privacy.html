# Privacy Policy Last updated: October 3, 2026 ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how this application handles information when the user connects their TikTok account and uses the application's automation features. ## 2. Information We May Process Depending on the features used, the application may process: * TikTok account information provided through TikTok's authorization system; * authentication and authorization information required to access permitted TikTok features; * video files and metadata submitted for publication; * text such as video titles, descriptions, captions, and hashtags; * information necessary to communicate with third-party AI and media-generation services. The application does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information that is not necessary for its operation. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used only to: * authenticate the user's TikTok account; * generate and process video content; * upload or publish content to TikTok when authorized by the user; * operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the application. ## 4. Sharing With Third Parties Information may be transmitted to third-party services that are necessary for the application's operation, such as AI, video-generation, hosting, or social-media services. The application does not sell personal information. Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. ## 5. Data Retention The application retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality. Temporary video files and processing data may be deleted after the relevant automation task has been completed. ## 6. TikTok Authorization The application uses TikTok's official authorization mechanisms. The user may revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through the relevant TikTok account settings. After authorization is revoked, the application will no longer be able to use the revoked permissions. ## 7. Security Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. ## 8. Children's Privacy The application is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be available at the same URL. ## 10. Contact For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the application owner through the contact method associated with the application.
